# TRACCR by DMG Official — Expense, Income & Car Mileage Tracker

**TRACK TODAY. BUILD TOMORROW.**  
`INCOME | EXPENSES | MILEAGE | INSIGHTS | CONTROL`

TRACCR is a modern, responsive web application by **DMG Official** designed to track expenses by category, record multiple income sources, monitor work & personal car mileage with automatic fuel efficiency (MPG / km/L) calculations, set financial & mileage goals, and export comprehensive reports.

---

## Key Features

1. **Dashboard Overview**:
   - Instant KPI cards for **Total Income**, **Total Expenses**, **Net Income**, and **Total Mileage** with month-over-month percentage badges.
   - **Income vs Expenses** multi-month bar chart & **Expense Breakdown** interactive donut chart.
   - **Recent Transactions** feed & **Mileage Overview** card showing Work vs. Personal miles and average MPG/km/L efficiency.
2. **Track Expenses by Category**:
   - Record expenses across categories (`Food & Drinks`, `Car`, `Shopping`, `Bills`, `Travel`, `Groceries`, `Rent`, `Entertainment`, `Other`).
   - Filter by category, search term, or date period.
3. **Record Income Sources**:
   - Log payroll, salary, freelance, side jobs, and investment income streams.
4. **Car Mileage & Fuel Efficiency Tracker**:
   - Log trips as **Work** or **Personal** with start/end locations, distance (`mi` or `km`), fuel used (`gal` or `L`), and fuel cost.
   - Automatically calculates **MPG** or **km/L**, cost per mile, and estimated IRS business mileage deduction.
5. **Reports & CSV Export**:
   - Detailed **Overview**, **Income**, **Expenses**, and **Mileage** report tabs with horizontal category breakdown bars and one-click CSV export.
6. **Goals & Milestones**:
   - Track savings targets, monthly expense caps, and personal mileage limits with real-time progress bars.
7. **Responsive Desktop & Mobile UI**:
   - Adaptive layout featuring a sleek dark sidebar on desktop and a mobile-first bottom navigation bar + dark-mode preview toggle.
